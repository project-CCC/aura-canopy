About Us

A sanctuary above the clouds

Aura Canopy was founded in 2026 by a collective of Filipino conservationists and astrophotography enthusiasts who wanted to prove that luxury travel can be entirely regenerative. Perched safely on the scenic mountain ridges of Don Salvador Benedicto, Negros Occidental, our sanctuary allows guests to immerse themselves in untouched upland nature without sacrificing premium amenities. Every dome is meticulously engineered to leave zero permanent impact on the pristine volcanic soil beneath it.

Mission: To deliver transformative luxury hospitality that protects the Philippine nocturnal ecosystem, promotes indigenous reforestation, and honors the natural heritage of Negros.

Vision: To establish Western Visayas as the premier sustainable astrotourism and off-grid luxury capital of the Philippines by 2030.

Target Market: Affluent, eco-conscious couples, wellness travelers, astrophotography enthusiasts, and guests seeking a quiet premium escape.

Why Don Salvador Benedicto?

Don Salvador Benedicto is a highland municipality in Negros Occidental known for its pine-covered ridges, cooler upland air, and cloud-layer views at dawn, and exceptionally dark skies ideal for stargazing. The area offers a rare combination of altitude, climate, and natural beauty that makes it a compelling location for off-grid eco-luxury accommodation. We do not claim exclusivity over this community — we simply celebrate its unique highland character.
